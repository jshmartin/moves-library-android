# Breaklog privacy policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Breaklog is a practice and video organizer for breakers. It works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no servers. The app does not have permission to use the internet.

## What Breaklog stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's private storage:

- your moves, categories, sets and practices
- your practice log (dates, durations, reps, notes)
- links to videos on your phone, and which move, set or practice each video belongs to

Breaklog does not collect, send or sell any of this. The developer cannot see it.

## Permissions

- **Camera and microphone:** only to record practice videos when you tap Record. The microphone is optional; without it, clips are silent. Recordings are saved to the Movies/Breaklog folder on your phone.
- **Photos and videos:** only to find videos you added if they were moved or renamed on your phone. You can choose to allow only selected videos.

You can turn off any of these in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

## Backups and sharing

Breaklog does not use Google cloud backup. If you turn on backups, Breaklog writes a backup file to a folder you pick. If you choose to send a backup or a video to another app (for example, to move to a new phone), it goes only where you send it, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Breaklog, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything the app stored. Your video files and any backup files you made are not deleted; remove those yourself if you want.

## Children

Breaklog is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
